HOW TO PLAY

Make the house your own

Choose furniture, wall art and little details from the room set carousel. Each room now has its own filtered dressing set, plus categories like big pieces, soft furnishings, ornaments, lighting and wall art. Tap an item and release to select it. While something is selected, panning is paused and dragging anywhere in the viewport moves that focused item, which makes small or awkwardly placed objects much easier to control. Tap elsewhere without dragging to deselect it and return to house panning. Pinch to zoom. Small objects can sit on tables and drawers and travel with the furniture underneath them. Select a lamp or ceiling light to adjust its brightness, switch the light itself on or off, and control its shadows. Ceiling fittings use broad downward spotlights, while table and floor lamps still spread light in all directions. Use the PLACE and SETUP tabs below the house to switch between furnishing controls and lighting setup. SETUP contains Day/Evening, independent Direct/Ambient/Indirect switches, direct-light colour, room-tinted ambient fill, selected-light controls and an experimental room-local SH probe grid. Each room uses 18 probes; ray sampling is refreshed after lighting or furniture changes, while each object receives only its locally interpolated SH coefficients at render time. Rotate or put away selected items, change each room’s walls, wallpaper and floor, and switch between day and evening lighting. Your house saves automatically on this device.
