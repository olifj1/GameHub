HOW TO PLAY

Make the house your own

Choose furniture, wall art and little details from the room set carousel. Each room now has its own filtered dressing set, plus categories like big pieces, soft furnishings, ornaments, lighting and wall art. Tap an item and release to select it, then drag that selected item to move it. Dragging anywhere except the currently selected item — including directly over other furniture — slides the view, so you never need to hunt for an empty patch of floor. Pinch to zoom. Small objects can sit on tables and drawers and travel with the furniture underneath them. Select a lamp or ceiling light to adjust its brightness and switch that light’s shadows on or off. Ceiling fittings use broad downward spotlights, while table and floor lamps still spread light in all directions. Use the scene-lighting control beside Day/Evening to tune direct light, room-tinted ambient fill and a lightweight AO pass for each preset. Ambient now adds a visible room-coloured diffuse fill, while AO adds restrained contact and corner darkening without extra bounce lights. Rotate or put away selected items, change each room’s walls, wallpaper and floor, and switch between day and evening lighting. Your house saves automatically on this device.
