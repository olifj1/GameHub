HOW TO PLAY

Make the house your own

Choose furniture and little details from the carousel. Tap an item once to select it, then drag the selected item to move it. Drag empty space to slide around the house and pinch to zoom. Small objects can sit on tables and drawers and travel with the furniture underneath them. Select a lamp to adjust its brightness. Rotate or put away selected items, change each room’s walls and floor, and switch between day and evening lighting. Your house saves automatically on this device.
