HOW TO PLAY

Make the house your own

Choose furniture from the carousel, then drag it around the current room. Drag empty space to slide around the dollhouse. Tap an item to select, rotate or put it away. Change each room’s walls and floor, and switch between day and evening lighting. Your house saves automatically on this device.
