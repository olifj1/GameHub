HOW TO PLAY

Laser Lab

Tap Mirror or Prism, then tap the board where you want to place it. Tap the laser or any placed optic to select it; drag placed optics to move them, then use the large rotate control below the board. The light updates live while you move or rotate.

Mirrors obey the angle of reflection: changing a mirror by a small amount changes the outgoing beam by twice that amount.

Medium prisms split white light into red and blue. Hard uses an RGB prism that makes red, green and blue branches. Route each colour through matching checkpoints and targets.
