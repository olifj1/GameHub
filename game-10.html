HOW TO PLAY

Laser Lab Freeform

Rotate the laser, then add mirrors and splitters anywhere on the board. Drag a piece to move it and drag its round handle to rotate it. The light updates while you move.

Mirrors obey the angle of reflection: changing a mirror by a small amount changes the outgoing beam by twice that amount.

A splitter sends a red beam straight on and reflects a blue beam. Coloured targets only light when the matching colour reaches them.
