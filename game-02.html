HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Explore mode lets you move through a full 24-hour day and see the analogue clock, digital time and day cycle together. In Test mode, read the target time and its time of day — for example, 8:35 in the morning or 7:45 in the evening. Move the clock hands using the Hour and Minute sliders, then press Check. The number readouts are hidden in Test mode so the analogue clock and day cycle are the things you have to use.
