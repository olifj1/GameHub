HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Choose a difficulty, build a sequence of commands, then press Run. The forklift drives one square for each movement command and turns on the spot. Medium adds one crate to collect and deliver. Hard adds two crates: pick up and deliver both to their marked delivery squares before reaching the finish.
