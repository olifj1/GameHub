HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Use Left and Right to rotate the ship and Thrust to fly. Land gently on each checkpoint before reaching the finish flag. You can land on any flat platform if the ship is upright and moving slowly enough; hit a surface too fast or at too much of an angle and you will crash. Flight tuning is available from the sliders button beside Reset.
