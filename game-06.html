HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Choose a difficulty, then use Left and Right to rotate the ship and Thrust to fly. Easy is a simple launch-to-landing course. Medium adds a checkpoint and stars. Hard has a longer, more complex route with several checkpoint landings and more stars. Checkpoints must all be landed on before the finish; stars are optional. You can land on any flat platform if the ship is upright and moving slowly enough.
