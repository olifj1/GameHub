HOW TO PLAY

Snake Maze

Guide the snake from START to EXIT with the four direction buttons. The snake moves automatically once you make your first move.

Collect optional gems for more points. Every time you collect one, another gem appears somewhere safe in the maze. Each gem also makes the snake two segments longer, so you can keep chasing points for as long as you dare.

Hit a wall or your own tail and the run ends. You cannot reverse directly into yourself. The EXIT stays open throughout, so choose when to stop taking risks and escape. Reaching the first set of gem targets earns the best star score; extra gems keep adding points.
