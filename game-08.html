HOW TO PLAY

Roller Coaster

Build a complete coaster from Start to End. Choose a track piece, then tap Add. Every piece joins smoothly to the end of the track.

Gentle curves change the slope gradually; Tight curves turn it twice as quickly. Ordinary track runs on momentum and gravity. Lift, Lift Up and Lift Down pull the cart at one steady, deliberately slow speed — ideal for building a hill before a drop, but they also slow the cart on descents.

Tap Play to test the design. A successful coaster earns a five-star result based on its length and excitement. Watch the live speed and G-load, then change the track and try again.
