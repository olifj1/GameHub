HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Level Designer: build a maze with a laser start, exits, checkpoints, walls, corner reflectors and splitters. Tap an exit again to cycle white, red and blue. Set the player inventory and 5-star par, then tap Play this level. Your solution corners and splitters are hidden during the test.

Play: guide the beam through the maze by placing diagonal corner reflectors. Hit every checkpoint and exit. Medium and Hard add a splitter: white light becomes a red straight beam and a blue reflected beam, and coloured exits only accept the matching colour.

Stars: solve at or below par for five stars. The maze has loops and alternative corridors, so finding a route is only the first step — try to find an efficient one.
