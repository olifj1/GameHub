HOW TO PLAY

Roller Coaster

Build a complete coaster from Start to End. Choose a track piece, then tap Add. Every piece joins smoothly to the end of the track.

Curve Up and Curve Down change the slope a little at a time. Ordinary track runs on momentum and gravity. Lift track pulls the cart at one steady, deliberately slow speed — useful for climbs, but it will also slow the cart on a descent.

Tap Play to test the design. A successful coaster earns a five-star result based on its length and excitement. Watch the live speed and G-load, then change the track and try again.
