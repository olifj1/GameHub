HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Level Designer: choose a grid size and place a laser, checkpoints, targets, splitters or blocks. Lasers rotate through up, right, down and left. Splitters have two orientations. Save a layout if you want to reuse it.

Play: generated puzzles appear automatically. Place mirrors to turn the beam through 90° and guide it through every checkpoint into the target. Mirrors only have the two useful orientations; there are no diagonal beam directions. Use New level for another puzzle.
