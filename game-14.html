HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Set up four deployment buttons with vehicles and routes. Start the attack, then launch vehicles whenever you choose; cash is spent when a vehicle deploys. Direct is shorter but more heavily defended. Winding is longer with fewer turrets. Pause returns to planning without resetting the battle, so you can remap your buttons and jump back in. When the field is clear, end the round to receive funding and face stronger defenders.
