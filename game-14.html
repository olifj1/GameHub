HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Both sides earn credits continuously. In Command, assign attacking units or defensive weapons to your four quick buttons. Return to battle, then drag an attacking unit onto one of the four routes to launch it. Drag a defence onto a highlighted emplacement to build it. Fast Scouts try to slip through, while armed vehicles stop to fight. Open Command at any time to freeze the battlefield, remap your quick buttons, then return to the same live match.
