HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Build an attack force with the cash available, choose the route for each vehicle, then send the wave. The Direct route is shorter but has more enemy turrets. The Winding route takes longer but is less heavily defended. Surviving vehicles damage the enemy base and earn extra cash. After every wave the defenders get stronger and you return here to plan the next attack.
