HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Choose Grand Prix or Time Trial in the driving settings, then press Go once and steer with Left and Right. Grand Prix runs for 1 lap on Easy, 2 on Medium and 3 on Hard. Time Trial keeps going so you can chase your best lap. You can switch the best-lap ghost on or off, choose Formula or Road handling, and keep separate tuning for each car. During a run the centre button becomes Pause; use the pause menu to resume or restart safely.
