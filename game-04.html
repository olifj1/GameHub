HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Read: choose a difficulty and read the sentence yourself. Use Read aloud if you want to hear it spoken, and choose a voice from the Voice menu.

Test: choose the word that correctly completes the sentence. Correct and incorrect answers are shown after you make a choice.

Use New Sentence or New Question whenever you want another one.
