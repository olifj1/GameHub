HOW TO PLAY

Instructions

Choose a difficulty, start the engine and use Up or Down to pitch the biplane. Fly through every numbered ring in the direction it points, collect any optional bonus stars, then make a gentle landing on the finish runway. Finishing gives you a five-star result rating, and collecting more bonus stars improves that rating and your Flight score. Your best result is saved separately for Easy, Medium and Hard. Easy has two simple rings; Medium adds more rings and angled approaches; Hard uses six rings including steep and vertical approaches. Climbing uses momentum, and if you run out of speed gravity will pull you back down so you can point downhill and recover.
