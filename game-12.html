HOW TO PLAY

Colour Blocks

The board is divided into four larger blocks. Your goal is to make every larger block a single solid colour.

Swipe left or right on any tile to shift its whole row by one place. Swipe up or down to shift its whole column. Tiles that move off one edge wrap around to the other side, without changing order.

It does not matter which colour ends up in which block. Every puzzle starts from a solved board and is scrambled using these same wrapped shifts, so there is always a way back.
